Fearsome Critters

cryptid Danger: unknown Evidence: unknown

Overview

Fearsome critters are a body of legendary tall-tale animals from the oral tradition of late-19th and early-20th century North American logging camps, ranging from the Hodag to the Hidebehind, the Squonk, the Hugag, and the Splintercat.

Details

Origin:
Fearsome critters are concentrated in the historical North American lumber regions: the Great Lakes pinewoods (Michigan, Wisconsin, Minnesota), New England, the Pacific Northwest, the Appalachians (especially Maryland and West Virginia), the Ozarks, and the Canadian boreal forest.
Regions:
Great Lakes, Wisconsin, Michigan, Minnesota, Pacific Northwest, Appalachian Mountains, Ozarks, Canadian boreal forest, Maine
First Reported:
1880-01-01
