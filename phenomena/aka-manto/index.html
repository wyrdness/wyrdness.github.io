Aka Manto

ghost_haunting Danger: unknown Evidence: unknown

Overview

Aka Manto ('Red Cloak') is a Japanese urban legend about a masked, red-cloaked spirit who haunts public and school toilets and asks the occupant to choose between red or blue toilet paper (or red or blue cloak). Either choice ends in death; only refusal lets the victim escape.

Details

Origin:
Originally a Japanese school-yard legend; widely retold across Japan and exported through pop culture to East Asia, North America, and beyond.
Regions:
Osaka, Tokyo, Kitakyushu, Korean Peninsula (under Japanese administration in 1940)
First Reported:
1930s
