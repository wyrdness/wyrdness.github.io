Full Account

Eyeless Jack is a fictional horror character that emerged from internet creepypasta culture, first appearing in 2012. He is typically depicted as a humanoid entity wearing a black hoodie and a blue mask, with empty eye sockets leaking black fluid, who stalks victims at night, breaks into their homes, and surgically removes and consumes their internal organs — most characteristically their kidneys. The character was created by a user on the Creepypasta Wiki (username Azelf5000) and published on February 25, 2012. A reference image associated with the character was originally posted on 4chan on June 7, 2010, by a user named Arnon as part of a thread about disturbing imagery from video games. Eyeless Jack became part of the informal 'creepypasta canon' of recurring internet horror characters, alongside Slender Man, Jeff the Killer, and others. Fan-created origin stories — most notably one by Kiki-Hyuga — elaborated the character's backstory significantly beyond the original story. The character has no basis in real events or sightings.

Cultural Significance

Within creepypasta fan communities, Eyeless Jack is treated as a shared fictional character with a loose canonical set of traits (blue mask, black hoodie, empty eyes, organ consumption). Some younger readers have interpreted creepypasta characters including Eyeless Jack as real.
