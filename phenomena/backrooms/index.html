The Backrooms

urban_legend Danger: unknown Evidence: unknown

Overview

An internet-born horror concept describing an endless extradimensional maze of empty yellow-walled office space, reached by 'no-clipping' out of reality. Originated as a single anonymous 4chan /x/ post on 12 May 2019 and grew into a sprawling collaborative fictional universe.

Details

Origin:
The Backrooms is a digital-era folkloric phenomenon distributed over the global Anglophone internet — primarily through 4chan, Reddit, YouTube, dedicated wikis (backrooms.wikidot.com, backrooms-wiki.fandom.com), and TikTok.
Regions:
Anglophone internet; English-language imageboard and wiki communities
First Reported:
2019-05-12

Full Account

The Backrooms is a collaborative internet horror phenomenon that originated on 12 May 2019, when an anonymous user posted a now-iconic image to 4chan's /x/ board in a thread asking for 'images that just feel off'. A second anonymous user responded with the description: 'If you're not careful and you noclip out of reality in the wrong areas, you'll end up in the Backrooms, where it's nothing but the stink of old moist carpet, the madness of mono-yellow, the endless background noise of fluorescent lights at maximum hum-buzz, and approximately six hundred million square miles of randomly segmented empty rooms to be trapped in.' The premise spread rapidly across Reddit, YouTube, and dedicated wikis, and was gradually elaborated into a multi-level fictional cosmology — 'Level 0' (the original yellow rooms) is one of hundreds of imagined levels with their own hostile entities, communities ('M.E.G.', 'B.N.T.G.', etc.), and rules. In January 2022 a then 16-year-old YouTuber named Kane Parsons (Kane Pixels) released 'The Backrooms (Found Footage)', a Blender-rendered VHS-style short that re-energised the concept and accumulated over 70 million views. On 6 February 2023 A24 announced a feature-length adaptation directed by Parsons, with James Wan producing through Atomic Monster. The originating photograph was identified in May 2024 by online investigators as a 12 June 2002 image of a HobbyTown USA store under renovation in Oshkosh, Wisconsin. The Backrooms is a prominent contemporary example of how 'liminal space' aesthetics — empty, transitional, and uncanny familiar places — combine with collaborative internet fiction to produce a new form of folkloric horror.

Cultural Significance

Treated by virtually all participants as collaborative fiction with no claim to literal truth, while drawing on the genuinely uncanny psychology of empty interior spaces.

Related Phenomena

  • Liminal spaces aesthetic — Broader online aesthetic of empty, transitional places that the Backrooms helped popularise.
  • SCP Foundation — Earlier collaborative-fiction wiki universe of anomalous phenomena.
  • Creepypasta — Genre of short internet horror fiction that the Backrooms emerged from.
  • Weirdcore — Adjacent visual aesthetic of off-kilter, low-fidelity images.
  • House of Leaves — Mark Z. Danielewski's 2000 novel about an impossibly larger interior space, often cited as a precursor.
