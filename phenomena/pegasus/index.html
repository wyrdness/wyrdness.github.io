Pegasus

mythological_creature Danger: unknown Evidence: unknown

Overview

Pegasus is the immortal winged horse of Greek mythology, sired by Poseidon and born from the blood of the Gorgon Medusa when Perseus beheaded her; the steed of the hero Bellerophon, later the bearer of Zeus's thunderbolts and the namesake of the constellation Pegasus.

Details

Origin:
As a literary and religious figure, Pegasus belongs to the Greek and wider Mediterranean mythological imagination, with a documented narrative geography centred on Corinth, Mount Helicon, Lycia, and Mount Olympus.
Regions:
Boeotia (Mount Helicon), Corinth and the Corinthian Gulf, Lycia
First Reported:
0700 BCE
