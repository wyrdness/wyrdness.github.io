Impundulu

entity_spirit Danger: unknown Evidence: unknown

Overview

The Impundulu (Lightning Bird) is a supernatural creature from Zulu, Xhosa, and Pondo folklore in South Africa that serves as a witch's familiar, summons lightning, and is said to possess vampiric qualities and the ability to take human form.

Details

Origin:
South Africa; primarily associated with Zulu, Xhosa, and Pondo peoples of the eastern and southeastern regions.
Regions:
KwaZulu-Natal, Eastern Cape, South African coastal and interior regions
First Reported:
Pre-colonial
