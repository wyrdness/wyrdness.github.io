Grey Aliens (Greys)

ufo_uap Danger: unknown Evidence: unknown

Overview

Greys are the most commonly reported alleged extraterrestrial entity in modern UFO and abduction literature: small, hairless humanoids with disproportionately large heads and large black eyes, central to the Betty and Barney Hill case (1961) and Whitley Strieber's Communion (1987).

Details

Origin:
Reports concentrated in the United States, with significant numbers in the United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, and continental Europe. Per C. D. B. Bryan's review, Greys account for ~73% of US alien-encounter reports, ~50% in Australia, ~48% in continental Europe, and ~12% in the UK.
Regions:
northeastern United States, American Southwest, rural Britain, Australian outback
First Reported:
1961-09-19
