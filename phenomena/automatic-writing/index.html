Automatic Writing

psychic_phenomena Danger: unknown Evidence: unknown

Overview

Automatic writing is the practice of writing without conscious volition, attributed by practitioners to spirit communication, the subconscious mind, or psychic faculties — and explained by scientists through the ideomotor phenomenon of unconscious muscular movement.

Details

Origin:
Automatic writing has been documented across multiple cultures and continents, with significant traditions in China (fuji/planchette writing), 16th-century European occultism, 19th-century Anglo-American Spiritualism, Brazilian Spiritism (kardecismo), and early 20th-century Surrealism.
Regions:
Global
First Reported:
