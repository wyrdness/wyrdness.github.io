Madremonte

entity_spirit Danger: unknown Evidence: unknown

Overview

Madremonte (Madre Monte, 'Mother Mountain' or 'Mother of the Forest') is a powerful female nature-spirit in Colombian folklore — protector of forests, rivers, and animals — who punishes those who damage the environment and is recognised across Andean Colombia and parts of the Caribbean coast.

Details

Origin:
Madremonte is most prominent in the Andean regions of Colombia — including Antioquia, the central and western Andes, the Eje Cafetero coffee-growing zones, and the Magdalena and Valle del Cauca river basins. Closely related figures (Fantasma del Monte, Madre Selva, Madre de los Cerros) appear in Argentina, Brazil and Paraguay.
Regions:
Colombian Andes, Antioquia, Eje Cafetero, Magdalena valley, Valle del Cauca
First Reported:
Pre-Hispanic
