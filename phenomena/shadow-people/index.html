Shadow People

urban_legend Danger: unknown Evidence: unknown

Overview

Shadow people are perceived dark, human-shaped silhouettes — often glimpsed in peripheral vision, in bedrooms during sleep paralysis, or at thresholds — and frequently associated with feelings of dread. Modern folklore consolidates a recognisable 'Hat Man' variant. Mainstream research attributes the experience to sleep paralysis, hypnagogic hallucination, and pareidolia rather than to external entities.

Details

Origin:
Reported worldwide. The modern internet-era reporting cluster is heavily English-language (United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia), but accounts have been collected from every inhabited continent. The phenomenon is a domestic-interior one; it is not concentrated in particular landscapes.
Regions:
First Reported:
Late 20th century (modern term)
