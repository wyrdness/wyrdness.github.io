Candle Cove

urban_legend Danger: unknown Evidence: unknown

Overview

A short horror fiction piece by webcomic author Kris Straub, published in 2009 on the Ichor Falls website. Written in the form of a forum thread in which adults remember a disturbing 1970s children's television program called 'Candle Cove' that turns out never to have existed.

Details

Origin:
An internet phenomenon distributed through the Ichor Falls site, creepypasta wikis, Reddit, YouTube readings, and the Channel Zero television adaptation. The fictional setting is a UHF children's television program in the Huntington–Ashland (West Virginia / Kentucky / Ohio) media market.
Regions:
Internet (global), Ohio River Valley (in-fiction setting)
First Reported:
2009
