Candle Cove

Overview

A short horror fiction piece by webcomic author Kris Straub, published in 2009 on the Ichor Falls website. Written in the form of a forum thread in which adults remember a disturbing 1970s children's television program called 'Candle Cove' that turns out never to have existed.

Full Account

Candle Cove is a creepypasta short story written by American webcomic author Kris Straub and published on his fiction site Ichor Falls in 2009. The piece is presented as a transcript of a NetNostalgia forum thread in which several adults from the Ohio River Valley share half-remembered fragments of a low-budget puppet show called Candle Cove that aired on a local UHF station (Channel 58) in the 1970s. The recollections grow progressively more disturbing — culminating in a 'Skin-Taker' marionette and an episode in which the puppets simply scream — before one user's mother delivers the closing twist: the television was tuned to static during those broadcasts. Candle Cove is widely cited as a foundational example of the creepypasta genre and was adapted as the first season of Syfy's anthology series Channel Zero in 2016. The work is a documented piece of fiction; Wyrdness catalogues it as an internet legend rather than a paranormal phenomenon.
