El Sombrerón

entity_spirit Danger: unknown Evidence: unknown

Overview

A small, dark-clad spirit of Guatemalan folklore wearing an oversized hat, who serenades and seduces young women with long hair, braids the manes of horses at night, and is one of the country's signature legendary figures alongside La Llorona, El Cadejo, and La Siguanaba.

Details

Origin:
Concentrated in Guatemala, particularly the highlands and traditional colonial neighborhoods of Guatemala City and Antigua. Variants and parallel legends are found across Mexico and other parts of Central America.
Regions:
Guatemalan highlands, Antigua Guatemala, Guatemala City (La Recolección, Parroquia Vieja), Quetzaltenango
First Reported:
1930-01-01
