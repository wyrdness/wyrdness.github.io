Teotihuacan

location Danger: unknown Evidence: unknown

Overview

Teotihuacan is a vast pre-Columbian city in the Valley of Mexico, approximately 40 km northeast of Mexico City, that was one of the largest cities in the ancient world at its peak (c. 100,000–200,000 inhabitants around 400–500 CE); its builders remain unknown, and the city features extraordinary astronomical alignments, a 100-meter ritual tunnel sealed for 2,000 years, and evidence of collective governance unlike other ancient civilizations.

Details

Origin:
Single location in the Valley of Mexico, but cultural influence extended across Mesoamerica during the city's peak period (1–650 CE).
Regions:
Valley of Mexico, State of Mexico (municipio de San Juan Teotihuacan)
