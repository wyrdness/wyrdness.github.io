Centaur

mythological_creature Danger: unknown Evidence: unknown

Overview

A creature of Greek mythology with the upper body of a human and the lower body and legs of a horse. Centaurs inhabited the mountains and forests of Thessaly and feature in some of the foundational stories of Greek myth, including the Centauromachy and the education of heroes by the wise centaur Chiron.

Details

Origin:
In Greek myth, centaurs inhabit the mountains of Thessaly (especially Mount Pelion) and the Foloi oak forest of Elis in the western Peloponnese.
Regions:
Thessaly, Mount Pelion, Mount Othrys, Foloi (Elis), Magnesia
First Reported:
0750
