Exorcism

Overview

A religious or spiritual practice for evicting demons, jinn, or other malevolent entities from a person, place, or object; recognized in Christianity, Judaism, Islam, Hinduism, and Buddhism, with the Catholic 'Roman Ritual' (1614, revised 1999) being the best-known formal rite.

Details

Origin:
Exorcism is practiced globally, in every major religious tradition, with regional and confessional variations.
Regions:
Roman Catholic world, Protestant evangelical world, Sunni and Shia Islam, Orthodox Judaism (Kabbalistic), Hindu South Asia, Tibetan Buddhist world
