Full Account

Lavender Town Syndrome is a creepypasta — a piece of internet horror fiction — that emerged in February 2010 and went on to become one of the canonical Pokémon-related online legends. The text alleges that the chiptune background music of Lavender Town in the 1996 Game Boy titles Pokémon Red and Pokémon Green (composed by Junichi Masuda) drove hundreds of Japanese children to suicide, headaches, brain haemorrhage and erratic behaviour through binaural beats and high-frequency tones audible only to children. The story originated as a Pastebin post on 21 February 2010, spread through 4chan's paranormal board (/x/), and was elaborated with photoshopped 'spectrograms' and fabricated newspaper references. No corresponding Japanese newspaper archive entries, coroner reports, or epidemiological records exist. The legend draws plausibility from the genuine in-universe creepiness of Lavender Town (a fictional graveyard town) and from confusion with the unrelated 1997 Dennō Senshi Porygon television incident, in which roughly 700 Japanese children were hospitalised after watching an episode of the Pokémon anime that contained a strobing red-and-blue sequence. Lavender Town Syndrome itself is uniformly classed as fiction; the Wikipedia article on Lavender Town treats it as 'an internet creepypasta' and notes that no underlying real event has been found.
