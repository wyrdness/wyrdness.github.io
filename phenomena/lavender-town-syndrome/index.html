Lavender Town Syndrome

urban_legend Danger: unknown Evidence: unknown

Overview

Lavender Town Syndrome is a creepypasta — an explicitly fictional internet horror story — first posted in February 2010 alleging that the music of Lavender Town in 1996's Pokémon Red and Green caused suicides and illness in Japanese children. No newspaper records or epidemiological evidence support the claim.

Details

Origin:
Internet-native phenomenon distributed through English- and Japanese-language creepypasta and Pokémon fan communities, with in-game setting in fictional Kanto, Japan.
Regions:
Pastebin, 4chan /x/, creepypasta wikis, YouTube

2010-02-21
