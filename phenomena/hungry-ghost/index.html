Hungry ghost

ghost_haunting Danger: unknown Evidence: unknown

Overview

Hungry ghosts (Sanskrit preta; Chinese e gui; Japanese gaki) are beings of one of the six Buddhist realms of rebirth, characterized by insatiable hunger and thirst caused by past karma of greed and attachment.

Details

Origin:
Hungry-ghost belief follows the geographic spread of Buddhism. Doctrinally rooted in South Asia (India, Nepal, Sri Lanka), it became a major popular tradition across China, Taiwan, Japan, Korea, Vietnam, Tibet, Mongolia, and Southeast Asia (Thailand, Myanmar, Cambodia, Laos).
Regions:
South Asia, East Asia, Southeast Asia, Tibetan plateau, Sinosphere, Theravāda Buddhist countries
First Reported:
c. 1st c. BCE
