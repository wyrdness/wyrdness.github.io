Chipekwe

cryptid Danger: unknown Evidence: unknown

Overview

The Chipekwe is a large, horned, hippo-killing aquatic cryptid reported from the swamps and rivers of Zambia and parts of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Bemba-language sources describe it as a 'water rhinoceros' with smooth hairless skin and a single white horn.

Details

Origin:
Centred on the wetlands of north-central and northeastern Zambia, particularly Lake Bangweulu and its associated swamps, the Kafue Flats, and the Luapula river system. Reports also extend into adjoining parts of the Democratic Republic of the Congo and to Lake Tanganyika in some 20th-century retellings.
Regions:
Lake Bangweulu, Bangweulu Swamps, Kafue Flats, Luapula River, Lake Mweru basin
First Reported:
1907
